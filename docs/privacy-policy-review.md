# Privacy Policy content review

The `/privacy-policy` route follows section 25 of the supplied content document and screenshot: information collected, purposes, enquiry sharing, retention, choices/rights, security and contact. Browser-storage, sign-in, location and upload descriptions were checked against the current frontend implementation. This is a content implementation, not certification of legal compliance or of backend data-handling controls.

Before public release, the business owner and qualified reviewer should confirm:

- The legal operator's name, address and appropriate privacy/grievance contact.
- Actual provider recipients, processor relationships and storage/transfer locations.
- Retention periods for account records, enquiries, verification material, uploaded documents and backups, plus how deletion requests are handled.
- Access controls, document security, identity-verification processes and complaint handling.
- The applicable legal framework, commencement provisions, user rights and consent requirements for the business and its audiences.

No unverified privacy email, fixed retention period, response deadline, data-sale claim or security certification was added. The page points users to the existing Contact route. The actual contact/privacy-request handling process should be reviewed as well.

Reference used for general notice and privacy-rights context: [Government of India backgrounder on the DPDP framework](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190655&lang=2&reg=48). The policy does not assume every provision is in force for every user or business.
